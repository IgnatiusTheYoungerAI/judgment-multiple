# CatLoop — Viral launch playbook (policy-safe)

Goal: first breakout app on your account without risking **5.6 Developer Code of Conduct** or review rejection.

---

## Product is the growth loop

1. User makes a clip in &lt; 60s  
2. Share sheet → TikTok / Reels / Shorts  
3. Soft end-card or optional caption: `Made with CatLoop`  
4. Viewer installs → same loop  

If the output isn’t share-native, ads won’t save it. Ship quality before spend.

---

## Compliant acquisition channels

| Channel | How | Don’t |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Organic social | Post best outputs; UGC creators with disclosure | Fake “organic” astroturf |
| Apple Search Ads | Brand + generic (`ai video`, `cat video`) | Competitor trademark bidding abuse if against ASA policies |
| Influencers | Paid seed with #ad / paid partnership | Fake reviews as part of deal |
| Product page | ASO: subtitle, screenshots, preview video | Keyword spam, trademark stuffing (2.3.7) |
| Share watermark | Optional, removable for paid if you want | Misleading “filmed on iPhone” |

---

## Hard bans (account-level risk)

- Buying installs or reviews  
- Review gating (“If you love us, 5 stars; if not, email support”)  
- Incentivized ratings for credits  
- Clone apps / spam listings (4.3)  
- Metadata that doesn’t match the binary  
- Dark-pattern paywalls (trial toggles, hidden prices)

One ban on CatLoop can burn the whole multi-app plan.

---

## Metrics that mean “viral” for this model

| Metric | Early target |
| --- | --- |
| Share rate per successful render | Rising week over week |
| Intro → paid week 2 renewal | ≥ ~25%+ (watch closely) |
| Contribution / paid week (typical) | Track vs $3+ target |
| ASA CPI vs LTV (after Apple + COGS) | LTV:CAC ≥ 3x before scaling |
| Refund / dispute rate | Stay low; kill creatives that bait |

Spend only after sandbox + production purchase + generation are stable.

---

## 14-day soft launch sequence

1. US storefront only, SBP on, ASA $50–100/day brand+generic  
2. Watch: crash-free, generation success %, paywall conversion, refunds  
3. Fix policy or UX issues before creative scale  
4. Turn on creator seeding once share rate is real  
5. Expand territories  

---

## Creative angles that match the product

- “My cat as a movie trailer”  
- Before/after photo → video  
- One-tap share to Reels  

Avoid: claiming live action, celebrity deepfakes, cruelty jokes that trip 1.1.
