CatLoop Privacy Policy

Effective: September 13, 2026
Operator: IgnatiusTheYoungerAI LLC

What we collect

  • Account identifiers (e.g. Sign in with Apple)
  • Photos and text prompts you submit for generation
  • Purchase / subscription status from Apple
  • Basic diagnostics and usage needed to run the service

AI processing

With your explicit in-app consent, we send your photo and prompt to a third-party video generation provider to create your clip. We do not use your content to train our own models unless we ask for separate opt-in later.

Sharing

We share data with: Apple (payments), our hosting/storage providers, and the named AI video provider. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention & deletion

You may delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete Account). We delete or anonymize associated personal data subject to legal and fraud-prevention needs.

Children

CatLoop is not directed to children under 13.
