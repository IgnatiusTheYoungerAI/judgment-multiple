## What the deck got wrong (supersede)

### House cat $1.95 / video · Lion $4.95 / 6 videos

| | House cat | Lion bundle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $1.95 / video | $4.95 / **6** videos → **$0.825 / video** |
| Net @ 15% Apple | **$1.66** | **$0.70 / video** |
| Planning COGS (5s × $0.10, 2 attempts) | **$1.00** | **$1.00** |
| Contribution / video | **+$0.66** | **−$0.30** |

The deck’s own insight — *“Lion-mode mix is the lever that moves Better → Best”* — is a **profit trap**. More Lion mix = more revenue-on-paper and **worse** contribution, because the bundle underprices compute.

At real 2026 video API rates, a 6-pack at $4.95 trains heavy users to buy the SKU that destroys margin. The “Best” 50K-download scenario ($282K net/year) is not a profit forecast; it’s a **gross sales** snapshot that ignores COGS entirely.

### Free-credit onboarding

Every free personal render is unfunded COGS + refund/support risk. Replace with **stock watermarked demo** → paywall → paid render (see launch bible).

### Consumables-only as the business

AI video apps that meter expensive GPUs need either:

1. **Subscription with hard credit caps** (predictable COGS; non-users still pay), or  
2. Consumables priced **above** worst-case COGS with no cheap multipacks.

CatLoop v1 chooses (1). Optional **Extra Video** consumable later at ~$2.99 — never a $0.83 effective video.
